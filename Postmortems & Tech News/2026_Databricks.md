Databricks' main product is the Databricks Data + AI Platform, a unified, open analytics platform built on a data lakehouse architecture. Key Capabilities of the Platform

- Data Lakehouse: Combines the flexibility and scale of data lakes with the structure and governance of enterprise data warehouses into a single system.
- Data Engineering & ETL: Uses Apache Spark, Delta Lake, and Lakeflow pipelines to clean, process, and move data at scale.
- AI and Machine Learning: Provides tools like MLflow and Databricks Runtime for Machine Learning to build, fine-tune, and deploy large language models (LLMs) and AI agents.
- Data Governance: Uses Unity Catalog to manage and govern data, analytics, and AI assets securely across clouds.
- SQL and Warehousing: Offers serverless data warehousing capabilities for business intelligence and analytics. [[2](https://www.databricks.com/company/about-us#:~:text=Product%20Databricks%20Data%20+%20AI%20Platform%20Platform,ETL%20and%20orchestration%20for%20batch%20and%20strea)]
