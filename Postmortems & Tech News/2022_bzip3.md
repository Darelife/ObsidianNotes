BZip3 was initially launched in 2022. Developed by Kamila Szewczyk (kspalaiologos), the open-source program was designed as a faster and more efficient "spiritual successor" to the classic BZip2 compression format. It utilizes a modern Burrows–Wheeler transform, an LZ77-style string matching pass, and a context-mixing entropy coder to deliver significantly higher compression ratios and faster performance, particularly for text and code.
